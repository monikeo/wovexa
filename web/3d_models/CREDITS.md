# 3D model library credits

Nine are by other people and shared under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0); they are credited below and in the studio wherever they are worn.

The other seven models (LongSleeve1, TaekwondoDobok, TShirt1, TShirt2, TShirt3, TShirt4 and TShirtLowPoly1) are the WOVEXA project's own.
