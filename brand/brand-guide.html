From Concept to Creation.

Brand guidelines for WOVEXA, the apparel design studio. Version 1.0, November 2026.

The symbol

A W woven from two threads.

The symbol is two V strokes laid side by side. Where they meet, the first passes over the second, the way a weft thread crosses the warp. That single crossing is what makes the W ours: it says woven without drawing fabric. Strokes are parallel-sided with flat, horizontal ends.

Wordmark and lockups

The letters are drawn, not typed.

The wordmark is built from the same straight strokes and flat ends as the symbol, with one round O. Use the horizontal lockup by default; use the stacked lockup in square spaces such as profile images and packaging tags.

Colour

Black and white carry the brand. Blue only shows the way.

The identity is black and white, so a garment's own colours always win. Wovexa Blue belongs to the product: it marks the active tool, the focused field and the one primary action on a screen. It never recolours the logo, with one exception: the blue app icon.

Typography

Archivo, stretched for a voice, normal for work.

One family does everything. Archivo's width axis lets headlines run wide, echoing the open stance of the wordmark, while interface text stays at normal width for density. Measurements always use tabular figures so columns of numbers line up. Khmer and Chinese text falls back to Noto Sans Khmer and Noto Sans SC.

Product modules

One studio, five rooms.

Module names pair the symbol with "Wovexa" in title case and a plain noun. They share one account and one project, so a design made in Studio is the same design in 3D, Render and Production.

  • Wovexa Studio

    The 2D editor: artwork, text, layers and print zones in real millimetres.

  • Wovexa 3D

    The live garment: every edit shown on the shirt as you make it, in any colourway.

  • Wovexa Render

    Product images and turntables for shops, lookbooks and social posts.

  • Wovexa Production

    Preflight checks, print files and tech packs locked to the approved version.

  • Wovexa AI

    Optional help, such as background removal, that you can always undo.

Before launch

An open item, not a formality.

A preliminary web search found no established apparel-design platform called WOVEXA. That is not trademark or domain clearance. Search the trademark registers in each market you plan to sell in, check the domain and social handles, and record the result before the name goes public.
